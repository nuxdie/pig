Pig

How to play

Roll as often as you dare — every roll adds to the line. Bank it and the points are yours. Roll a single 1 and the line is struck out; roll two 1s and your balance is wiped to zero. First to 100 wins. Cross 25, 50 and 75 to draft a card — tin, then silver, then gold.
