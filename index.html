Pig

Roll two dice as often as you dare — every roll adds to the line. Bank it and the points are yours. Roll a single 1 and the line is struck out; roll two 1s and your balance is wiped to zero. First to 100 wins. Cross 25, 50 or 75 and you draft a card — a weighted die, a standing rule, or a one-use charge.
